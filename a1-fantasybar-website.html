Policies

Please Read Before Your Appointment

A quick guide to how appointments, deposits, and hair prep work at A-1 Fantasybar — please review before booking.

NO-DEPOSIT = NO APPOINTMENT!!!

Deposits & Payments

  • A $25 deposit is required to secure your appointment.
  • Paying via Cash App: $27.00.
  • Deposits are non-refundable.
  • Your deposit is deducted from the service price. The remaining balance is due the day of your appointment.

Cancellations

  • 24-hour notice is required for cancellations.
  • To cancel, text (469) 463-8438.

Appointment Guidelines

  • No kids, no extra people please!!
  • I do not take down hair. If a takedown is needed, a $40 service charge will be added.

Hair Preparation

  • Bundles, wigs, frontals, and closures for customization must be dropped off 2 days prior to your appointment.
  • Items not dropped off in advance are subject to a $75 same-day service fee.
